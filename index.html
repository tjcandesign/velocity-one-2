Velocity One is a private industrial platform focused on acquiring and growing lower middle-market companies in the aerospace, defense, and industrial sectors. We partner with exceptional management teams to build market-leading businesses through operational excellence and strategic investments.

Our approach combines deep industry expertise with a long-term investment horizon, enabling our companies to achieve their full potential while maintaining their entrepreneurial spirit.
